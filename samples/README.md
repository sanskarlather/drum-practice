# Samples

Small files for trying out, and testing, the importers.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `rock-beat.mid` | 2 bars of an 8th-note rock beat on MIDI channel 10, 120 BPM |
| `rock-beat.musicxml` | 1 bar of the same beat in MusicXML drum notation, 96 BPM |
| `rock-beat-sheet.pdf` | A printed page with 4 bars of an 8th-note rock beat, for trying the sheet readers |

In the app: **Library → MIDI file**, **MusicXML** or **PDF or photo**, then pick the file.

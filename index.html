How practice works
  1. Press play. You get a 4-click count-in.
  2. The drum to hit glows. A ring closes in on it: hit when the ring touches the drum.
  3. The tag on each drum says which hand or foot to use.
  4. Turn on Wait for me to go at your own pace.
Sheet reading

PDFs and photos are read by one of two readers. Either way, the notes open in the editor so you can check them.

  • AI reader: most accurate, handles photos. Uses your own key from whichever AI service you have access to: Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Alibaba Cloud (Qwen), Mistral, Hugging Face, Anthropic Claude, or any OpenAI-compatible service you add.
  • Offline reader: free and private. Best on clean, printed sheets with simple rhythms.


Keys are stored only in this browser, sent only to the provider you chose, and never part of the app or its GitHub repository. Anyone else using the app adds their own key.

Microphone mode

For an acoustic kit, or when the e-kit can't connect. The app listens to your hits and checks your timing, and, once you've taught it your drums, which drum you hit. Timing is reliable; telling similar drums apart (two toms, crash vs ride) is not perfect.

Raise it if soft hits are missed; lower it if it hears things you didn't play. Last heard: —

1. Teach it your drums

Press Teach, then hit that drum 6 times at normal strength, about a second apart. Only teach the drums you have. Teach again if you move the mic or change rooms. Also teach the open hi-hat, rimshot, cross-stick and ride bell if you want those checked; accents and ghost notes are judged by how loud you hit compared with your normal hits.

3. Measure the delay

Turn your speakers up and press the button: the app plays 8 clicks and times how long the mic takes to hear them.

While practising: use headphones for the metronome, otherwise the mic hears the clicks as hits. On an e-kit, mic mode only works if the module plays through a speaker near the mic.

Pad mapping

If a pad lights the wrong drum, press Learn and then hit that pad.

Timing

If every hit says "late" even when it sounds right, move this left until most hits read "Perfect". Bluetooth headphones add a lot of delay; wired headphones from the module are best.

Accents and ghost notes

How hard you hit (MIDI velocity 1–127) decides whether an accent or ghost note counts. Hit a pad and watch Last note received to find your levels.
